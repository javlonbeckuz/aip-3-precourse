# Accessibility audit (WCAG 2.2 AA): AI Praktikum 3.0 student platform

- **Audited:** `AI-Praktikum-3.0-final.html` (file://) on 2026-10-19, in headless Chrome over CDP (`.design/comp/src/tests/cdp_sc.mjs`).
- **Viewports:**
  - 390×844 with mobile emulation, plus 320×640;
  - 1024×768 and 1440×900;
  - zoom equivalents: 640×400 (200% of 1280×800) and 320×256 (400% reflow).
- **States:**
  - all 42 crawl routes (`ROUTES()`) at `?today=2026-10-12`;
  - day 0 (`2026-09-05`) and the live lesson (`2026-09-26`), both with empty progress;
  - after a real login (popup), name menu, locked sheet, copy and toast, library search, slide grid, prep accordion;
  - UZ, RU and EN.
- **Method:**
  - **Contrast, computed:** every visible text node on every route was composited through its ancestor backgrounds and opacity, at 320, 390, 1024 and 1440.
  - **Contrast, measured on pixels:** for text over the photo or the gooey folder, I took two screenshots, with the text shown and hidden, and compared the text colour with the real background under each glyph pixel.
  - **Keyboard only:** CDP `Input.dispatchKeyEvent` with Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space, Escape, the arrows, Home and End. At each stop I logged the outline, the ring contrast and how much of the element was covered (`elementFromPoint` at 9 points).
  - **Accessible names:** from `Accessibility.getFullAXTree`.
  - **Reduced motion:** `Emulation.setEmulatedMedia`, then `document.getAnimations()`.
  - **Text spacing (1.4.12):** the standard bookmarklet values.
- **Not repeated:**
  - findings already open in `ux-pass-2.md`, `redundancy-audit.md`, `element-audit.md` and `color-audit.md`;
  - motion craft, which is in `motion.md`. I raise only its WCAG side (#10).
- **Respected, not re-proposed:**
  - lesson colours;
  - no glows;
  - no Telegram for students;
  - Uzbek course material;
  - copy = done;
  - the hero card;
  - ☰;
  - the login photo;
  - no slop decoration.
- **Screenshots:** `C:/Users/User/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-User-vs-code-HEAD-projects-praktikum3-precourse-platform/7b93abd2-e86f-4f93-b0fc-efc57770bbb1/scratchpad/audit5/a11y/`, written below as `a/…`. The probe scripts (`scan.js`, `pix.mjs`, `kb.mjs`, `i_*.mjs`) are in the same folder.
- **Tags:** **SAFE** = conformance or usability only. **LOOK** = changes what the screen looks like, so the user decides.

**Verdict: issues found, nothing that blocks a student.**
- The flat colour system passes everywhere. 0 text fails on 42 routes × 4 widths; the lowest pair is `text-3` data at 6.12:1.
- Every failure is in one of three places:
  - the **login photo**;
  - the **two fixed overlays** (the popup and the toast);
  - **focus rings that take `currentColor` or sit inside an SVG filter**.

## High

### 1 · The login button has no visible focus: the gooey filter erases its ring · `#/kirish`
- **Measured:**
  - `.mx-btn:focus-visible` computes `outline: 2px solid #fff; offset 3px`.
  - Its parent `.mx-submit` has `filter:url(#gooey)`, which blurs and alpha-thresholds the 2px ring away. In the screenshots the focused and unfocused button are pixel-identical, at 390 and at 1440.
  - Keyboard path: ID field → **KIRISH (invisible)** → UZ → RU → EN.
- **Why it matters:** this is the one button on the first screen. A keyboard or switch user can't tell that they are on it.
- **Fix (SAFE):** draw the ring inside the white pill, where the filter keeps it (it is opaque). It needs no new colour:
  ```css
  .mx-btn:focus-visible{outline:2px solid #000;outline-offset:-6px}
  ```
- **Screenshot:** `a/z_kb_gate390_2.png`, `a/z_gate1440_btnfocus.png` (focused)
- **WCAG:** 2.4.7 Focus Visible (AA), 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast (AA)

### 2 · The "Yangi dars ochildi" popup hides the focused Home button on desktop, and covers 74% of the screen at 400% zoom · `#/dastur` after login (2026-09-26)
- **Measured:**
  - At 1024 and 1440 the popup (`aside.nl`, fixed, top-left, 380×138) sits exactly over the home button.
  - Tabbing to "Bosh sahifa" puts focus under it: 9/9 sample points are covered, and no ring shows.
  - At 320×256 (400% zoom) it covers 74% of the viewport (212 of 256 px). "Yopish" is cut off at the bottom edge, and you can't scroll to it because the popup is fixed.
- **Why it matters:**
  - The Home button is the main way back, and the popup stays until it is dismissed.
  - Low-vision users on zoom get a page that is three-quarters popup.
- **Fix (SAFE):**
  - On desktop, anchor the popup under the nav instead of over it: `top: calc(var(--nav-h) + 16px)`. At 1440 that is 136px, clear of the 120px nav.
  - Add `@media (max-height:500px){.nl{position:static;margin:8px 16px}}` so that at high zoom it flows in the page and scrolls.
- **Screenshot:** `a/i_popup_obscured_1440_11.png`, `a/i_popup_zoom_320x256.png`
- **WCAG:** 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (AA), 1.4.10 Reflow (AA)

### 3 · The toast covers the focused prompt row · prompt lists, e.g. `#/dars/2/amaliyot/01-biznes-goyalar`
- **Measured:**
  - After a copy, the toast (fixed bottom, 48px high, plus 16px margin) stays 5 s.
  - Tabbing forward, the browser scrolls each stop just into view, so a 44px accordion button lands under the toast. "Kichik jamoa uchun biznes" was covered at 9/9 points; only the ring's corners peek out around the toast.
  - At 390 this happened on every 3rd–4th stop.
- **Why it matters:** the student loses track of where they are, mid-list, which is exactly where copying happens.
- **Fix (SAFE):** reserve the toast's band for scrolling, with no visual change:
  ```css
  html{scroll-padding-bottom:88px}
  ```
  Add a matching `scroll-padding-top` while the popup is open (see #2).
- **Screenshot:** `a/i_copy_obscured_390x844_7.png`
- **WCAG:** 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (AA)

### 4 · The example ID under the login field is unreadable on phones · `#/kirish`
- **Measured, glyph pixels of "Masalan: AIP-7KQ2XM":** Martian 11px, white at 85%.

  | Viewport | Below 4.5:1 | Below 3:1 | Lowest |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 320 | 31.9% | 17.7% | 1.29:1 |
  | 390 | 2.5% | – | 1.75:1 |
  | 1024 / 1440 | – | – | 4.3 / 2.3:1, but p5 ≥ 5.2 (fine) |

  - On narrow phones, "7KQ2XM" sits on the bright wave and the lit monitor.
- **Why it matters:** this line is the only explanation of the ID format, and the error state replaces it. It is small text, read on a phone, outdoors.
- **Fix (SAFE, existing pattern):** give `.mx-help` in every state the same 5-layer black halo that `.mx-help.err` already has. That halo measures ≥ 6.9:1 on the same photo (color-audit). Also raise its alpha from .85 to 1.
- **Screenshot:** `a/z_gate_320.png`
- **WCAG:** 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) (AA)

## Medium

### 5 · The login headline over the bright wave fails large-text contrast on phones · `#/kirish`
- **Measured:** "SHAXSIY KABINET" (48px, so 3:1 applies) has no text-shadow.
  - At 390, 6.0% of glyph pixels are below 3:1 (lowest 1.15:1).
  - At 320, 7.3% are below 3:1.
  - RU "ЛИЧНЫЙ КАБИНЕТ" is the same.
  - At 1024 and 1440 it passes: the lowest pixel is 5.4:1.
- **Why it matters:** the right half of "SHAXSIY" and "KABINET" dissolves into the wave on the phones most students use.
- **Fix (LOOK, small):** reuse the error halo at low strength on `h1` (`text-shadow: 0 0 12px rgb(0 0 0/.6), 0 0 28px rgb(0 0 0/.5)`). The photo stays as approved.
- **Screenshot:** `a/z_gate_320.png`, `a/gate_390.png`
- **WCAG:** 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) (AA)

### 6 · Typing the 6th character logs you in without warning · `#/kirish`
- **Measured:**
  - Typing `7KQ2XM` submits on the 6th keystroke, with no Enter and no button.
  - 150ms later the loader runs, and at 1.8 s the page changes to `#/dastur`.
  - The help text ("Masalan: AIP-7KQ2XM") does not say this will happen.
- **Why it matters:**
  - Screen-reader users who type and then review the field, or who use speech or switch input, are moved to a new page they did not ask for.
  - It is 2.0 behaviour (blueprint §2), so keep it, but say it beforehand. Advance notice is what satisfies 3.2.2.
- **Fix (SAFE, copy only):**
  - Help line: "Masalan: AIP-7KQ2XM · 6 ta belgi yozilsa, oʻzi kiradi."
  - Add the same sentence in RU/EN.
- **Screenshot:** `a/gate_390.png`
- **WCAG:** 3.2.2 On Input (A)

### 7 · The popup's keyboard path: the "Darsni ochish" ring is white on white, and the popup comes last in the Tab order · `#/dastur` after login
- **Measured:**
  - `.nl :focus-visible{outline-color:currentColor}`. On the black pill "Darsni ochish" the current colour is white, so the ring is white on the white card: **1.0:1, invisible**.
  - "Yopish" is fine: black, 21:1.
  - The popup is visually first (top of the screen), but it is reached at Tab stop 8, after the hero, the next step and all five lesson cards.
  - The polite announcement "Yangi dars ochildi: 3-dars…" is good.
- **Why it matters:** a keyboard user sees the popup first, has to tab through the whole page to reach it, and then can't see which of its two buttons has focus.
- **Fix (SAFE):**
  - `.nl :focus-visible{outline-color:#000}`. The ring sits on the white card, so black gives 21:1.
  - Insert `aside.nl` in the DOM right after the nav, so it is Tab stop 3. Its position stays fixed.
- **Screenshot:** `a/z_i_nlgo_focus_390.png`
- **WCAG:** 2.4.7 Focus Visible (AA), 2.4.3 Focus Order (A)

### 8 · "Bekor qilish" in the copy toast can't be reached by keyboard before it disappears · `#/dars/2/amaliyot/*`
- **Measured:**
  - The toast lives at the end of `<body>`, after `#nmenu`.
  - From the copy button it took **22 Tab presses**, and 4.6 s even at machine speed, to reach "Bekor qilish". The toast hides at 5 s and pauses only on hover or focus, which a keyboard user can't reach in time.
  - The live region tells screen-reader users "Bekor qilish tugmasi pastda".
  - A library prompt has no other way to un-mark it.
- **Why it matters:** a keyboard or screen-reader user who copies by mistake can't undo it, and the announcement points them at a button they can't reach.
- **Fix (SAFE):** move the toast node to just after the activated copy button (`li.after(toast)`). Its fixed position stays the same, so the next Tab lands on "Bekor qilish". The existing pause-on-focus then holds it.
- **Screenshot:** `a/i_copy_390x844.png`
- **WCAG:** 2.2.1 Timing Adjustable (A), 2.1.1 Keyboard (A)

### 9 · An untranslated key "⟦hc.no_prompts⟧" is shown and read aloud · `#/dars/4/amaliyot/04` (Hermes · Telegram)
- **Measured:**
  - The lilac section's eyebrow `#pr-t` renders the raw key, in every language.
  - `#pr-t` labels the region, so a screen reader announces "region ⟦hc.no_prompts⟧".
- **Why it matters:** the region's label is a code. A sighted student sees a bug.
- **Fix (SAFE):** add `hc.no_prompts` to `i18n_{uz,ru,en}.json`, e.g. "Bu kartada nusxalanadigan prompt yoʻq". Or drop the eyebrow and `aria-labelledby` when a card has no prompts. Then add the key to `i18n_check.py`.
- **Screenshot:** `a/h04_390.png`
- **WCAG:** 2.4.6 Headings and Labels (AA), 1.3.1 Info and Relationships (A)

### 10 · The login wordmark animates forever with no way to stop it · `#/kirish`
- **Measured:**
  - `.wm-g{animation:wm-wave 1.35s step-end infinite alternate}` runs on all 16 letters, `iterations=Infinity`.
  - Reduced motion stops it correctly: 0 animations under `reduce`.
  - Anyone without that OS setting gets an endless weight pulse beside the one field they have to fill in.
- **Why it matters:**
  - Moving content that starts by itself and lasts more than 5 s needs a pause, unless it stops on its own.
  - It is also against the tokens' own rule, "Nothing loops".
  - `motion.md` #12 proposes pausing it while the ID field has focus. That helps, but it does not meet 2.2.2.
- **Fix (LOOK):** `animation-iteration-count:3`. That runs about 4 s and stops, keeping the entrance.
- **Screenshot:** `a/gate_390.png`
- **WCAG:** 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide (A)

## Checked and passing (not findings)
- **Reflow at 320:** no horizontal scroll on any of the 42 routes, in UZ, RU or EN. At 640×400 (200% zoom) there is no content loss.
- **Dragging (2.5.7):** there are no drag interactions. Slides use buttons, ←/→ keys and a grid.
- **Target size (2.5.8):** apart from the back link (#17), nothing interactive is under 24px. The prep chip is 28px; the copy control is 70×65.
- **Language of page and parts:**
  - `<html lang>` follows the UI (uz-Latn, ru, en).
  - Course material carries `lang="uz-Latn"` inside RU and EN.
  - Language buttons carry their own `lang`.
- **Form errors:** `aria-invalid`, the message is in `aria-describedby` plus a polite live region, the text says what to do, and the field keeps focus.
- **Overlays:**
  - Text contrast: name menu at least 8.63, locked sheet at least 7.43, popup at least 14.56.
  - The sheet is a native modal `<dialog>`: focus moves in, Esc closes it, focus returns to the card.
  - The name menu: Esc closes it and returns focus to ☰ or the name, `aria-expanded` toggles, and Tab out closes it.

## Strengths (keep)
1. **The flat colour system is fully AA, with numbers.**
   - 0 text failures over 42 routes × 320, 390, 1024 and 1440, in both computed and pixel-sampled passes.
   - The lowest pairs are `text-3` data at 6.12:1 and ink-2 on lilac at 6.21:1. The gooey folder tabs measure at least 7.8:1.
   - State never relies on colour: ✓ plus a word, "· bajarildi" in sr-only, and the selected tab is a shape and fill change.
2. **Reduced motion is honoured completely.**
   - With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` there are 0 running animations on the gate, the welcome loader, home, prep, lessons and prompts.
   - Normally there are 21 on the loader alone.
   - The progress ring jumps to its final value.
3. **Widget semantics are done properly.**
   - **Folder tabs:** `role=tab` with roving tabindex and ←/→/Home/End. Inactive panes are `inert`, and the ring is visible in each lesson's ink.
   - **Route changes:** each one updates `<title>` and focuses the h1.
   - **Live regions:** copy, mark-done, search results ("5 ta natija") and the new-lesson popup are all announced through polite live regions.
   - **Names:** the copy buttons name their prompt ("Nusxalash: Bitta manbali javob"), and the download rows name file, type and size.
