AI Praktikum 3.0 · student platform · 19 Oct 2026

Design audit

Five expert lenses ran over every screen of the platform: 42 routes at 320, 390, 1024 and 1440 px, in Uzbek and Russian, on day 0, a live lesson and the all-open course, with empty and finished progress. 96 findings came back. This page merges them into 40 distinct issues, ranked.

Verdict
Not ready to ship yet

The look is on-brief and reads as Praktikum, not a template, and colour contrast passes everywhere. Three small bugs block release, and about a day of usability fixes would make it feel finished.

Fix first

Bugs a student would notice today. Each is a one-line or one-rule fix, and two of them are already in the real site's code.

  1. A raw code key shows on Hermes card 04

    #/dars/4/amaliyot/04 · every language and width

    The prompt area reads ⟦HC.NO_PROMPTS⟧, and screen readers announce it as the region name. The text was deleted during the redundancy clean-up, but the code still asks for it.

    Fix: render nothing when a card has no prompts, and add a build check that fails on any ⟦ in rendered text.

    UXAccessibility

  2. A stray "}" prints under the book rows

    #/dars/4 · Kitob tab · also in public/aip/app.js

    A template typo (.join('')}}) shows a lone brace on the finished screen, in both the prototype and the shipping site.

    Fix: delete the extra brace in both files, and add a crawl check for stray braces.

    Finish

  3. Hermes prompts are squeezed, and card 10 runs off the screen

    #/dars/4/amaliyot/01–10 · 320 and 390

    The copy button keeps its own column, so prompt text gets about 260 px (28–30 characters per line). On card 10 a long URL pushes text to x=449 on a 390 screen, and the copy circle sits on top of the words. The notes show raw backticks, and [GEMINI_API_KEY] is styled like a placeholder to replace, which would break the config.

    Fix: use the exercise-page row (copy circle beside the title, text full width below), add overflow-wrap:anywhere, show backtick spans as code, and skip placeholder styling inside code.

    FinishVisual

  4. The slide viewer breaks on small phones

    #/dars/n/slayd/last · 320 Russian

    The "back to lesson" control and the "All slides" pill are clipped. The stage is a tall portrait box while real slides are 16:9, and the real site shows an empty box while a slide loads, or a broken image if it fails.

    Fix: a three-column footer that can shrink, aspect-ratio:16/9 on the stage, and a loading state plus "Slayd yuklanmadi · Qayta urinish".

    FinishVisual

  5. The login screen fails keyboard and phone users

    #/kirish

    KIRISH has no visible focus ring, because the gooey filter erases it. The example line "Masalan: AIP-7KQ2XM" is 11 px on the bright photo, and at 320 a third of its letters fall below 4.5:1.

    Fix: draw the focus ring inside the white pill, and give the example line the black halo the error message already uses.

    Accessibility

Do next

Usability fixes that don't change the look. They reuse patterns the platform already has, so no taste sign-off is needed.

  1. On lesson day, the main card doesn't lead to today's work

    #/dastur · 12 and 26 Sept

    The hero says "Slaydlar · boshlash" while class is doing exercise 01, which is 3 taps away. "Keyingi qadam" points at an older lesson, and every lesson page opens on Slaydlar.

    Fix: the hero links to the live lesson's real next step, the block below becomes "Qolib ketgan ish", and lesson pages open on the tab that holds the next step.

    UX

  2. No link to NotebookLM or Claude anywhere

    exercises · Vazifa · library

    Seven pages say "upload to NotebookLM" and none links to it. The "new notebook per exercise" rule lives only on the index, which deep links skip.

    Fix: add "NotebookLMʼni ochish ↗" with the line "Yangi notebook oching va faqat shu fayllarni yuklang." under each upload step, and "Claudeʼni ochish ↗" in the library.

    UX

  3. 40 of 47 library prompts are copied with unseen [brackets]

    #/dars/2/amaliyot/*

    Rows are collapsed, so students paste "[Soha]" into Claude unfilled and get a generic answer.

    Fix: when a copied prompt has brackets, the toast says "Claudeʼda [qavslar]ni oʻz maʼlumotingiz bilan almashtiring."

    UX

  4. Taps give no designed feedback on phones

    all screens · 390

    There are no press states. Android shows a square cyan box, even through the sheet a tap opens, and hover sticks after a tap: the copy circle stays black next to "Nusxalash".

    Fix: remove the tap highlight, add a 100 ms scale(.96) press on circles and pills, and limit hover styles to mouse devices.

    Motion

  5. Switching folder tabs is slow and uses a blur

    #/dars/1–4

    The old pane fades out first, the folder is blank for about 50 ms, and the new content is readable only at about 270 ms, through an 8 px blur reveal. On a mid-range laptop the gooey filter drops to about 50 fps.

    Fix: one 200 ms crossfade with no blur, the indicator at 260 ms, and no animation when switching tabs with arrow keys.

    Motion

  6. The popup and toast hide what has focus

    #/dastur after login · prompt lists

    On desktop the "Yangi dars ochildi" popup covers the Home button. At 400% zoom it fills 74% of the screen. The copy toast covers the focused prompt, and "Bekor qilish" is 22 Tab presses away, so keyboard users can't undo.

    Fix: place the popup under the nav, reserve the toast's band with scroll-padding-bottom, and move the toast next to the button that triggered it.

    Accessibility

  7. The first and last screens look unfinished

    #/dastur · #/dars/0

    At 0/4 the ring paints a yellow dot that reads as progress. At 4/4 the course has no "done" message. Finishing prep shows nothing and needs a 1,700 px scroll back. After day 1 the prep page still says "Birinchi darsgacha", and unfinished prep is never suggested.

    Fix: hide the arc at 0; add a done line and "Tayyorsiz" endings using the existing next-step block; and send students with no Google or Claude account back to prep.

    FinishUXMotion

  8. Mono type is used for sentences

    Hermes index · 03A/03B picker · welcome

    The Hermes "Natija" lines and the exercise choices are full sentences in small grey Martian Mono. They read like a terminal log, and the type rule says mono is for data only.

    Fix: set them in Golos 15 px, and keep mono for counts, sizes and IDs.

    VisualFinish

  9. The type scale has drifted

    all screens

    24 sizes are in use where the tokens define 8. Labels, copy captions and 320 px tabs are 11 px, below the 13 px minimum. Lesson 4's headline (52 px) is smaller than its own sub-pages (56 px). 68 headings and intros end on a single orphaned word.

    Fix: snap to the token scale, make 13 px the floor, set long lesson titles at 64 px, and add text-wrap:balance to headings.

    VisualFinish

  10. The same things have different names

    home · lessons · Vazifa · gate

    "qadam" means four things. One exercise is called "Amaliyot 01", "MASHQ 01" and "Asosiy mashq". There are six labels for one done checkbox, three spellings of "manba", and "13 prompts" above a "0/8" counter. Typing 3 characters of an ID says "not found", and the login submits on the 6th character without saying so.

    Fix: use one word per meaning ("bajarildi" for counts, "Mashq" for exercises, "Manbalar"); "ID 6 ta belgidan iborat" for short IDs; and a help line that says it logs in on its own.

    UXAccessibility

  11. Deep pages lose their place, and the back link is tiny

    exercises · Hermes cards · library sections

    "← AMALIYOT" doesn't say which lesson, and the back link is 17 px tall on phones, though it is the main way back.

    Fix: labels like "1-dars · Amaliyot", plus an invisible 44 px hit area.

    UXAccessibility

  12. A failed copy is silent

    every prompt list

    When the clipboard is refused (some in-app browsers on iOS), nothing visible happens and nothing is marked.

    Fix: a toast saying "Nusxa olinmadi. Matnni bosib turing va «Nusxalash»ni tanlang."

    UX

  13. Downloads are easy to mix up

    exercise pages · Vazifa · Kitob

    Six different briefs are all called "01_Yoriqnoma.pdf", and two different files are both "Talaba_qollanmasi.pdf". The book rows use the → "go to page" pattern instead of the ↓ download circle. Long file names break mid-word.

    Fix: unique download names from the server, the standard file row for the book, and line breaks after underscores.

    UXFinish

  14. Alignment slips on desktop and small phones

    home · exercises · prep · amaliyot index

    Home card titles sit at different heights in one row. The "Keyingi mashq" link floats mid-column. Notes run 110 characters per line. Prep card footers are ragged. Row titles break one word per line at 320, and one list uses two row designs.

    Fix: one baseline per card row, a two-ended end row, 62ch notes, stretched prep cards, meta under the title below 400 px, and one row component.

    VisualFinish

  15. Things jump instead of moving

    prep · sheets · menu · home ring

    Ticking a prep card jumps the next one 85 px in one frame. Bottom sheets appear with no rise. The name menu stays put while the page scrolls away. The ring redraws for a full second on every visit to home, even after Back.

    Fix: a View Transition on prep, a 260 ms sheet rise, close the menu on scroll, and animate the ring only when progress went up.

    Motion

  16. Route changes stall on mid-range phones

    #/dars/1/amaliyot/01 and others · 4× CPU

    Opening an exercise is a 238 ms long task, 182 ms of it layout, with no press feedback to cover it.

    Fix: content-visibility:auto on off-screen rows. Target: 100 ms or less.

    Motion

  17. A glow is still on prompt sections

    every lilac prompt list

    A faint radial lilac glow (.s1::before) survived the glow removal you asked for.

    Fix: delete the rule.

    Finish

  18. Screen reader and keyboard details

    library · prep · slides · login

    Each of the 47 library prompts is announced ending in "plus". Four prep toggles share one name. The slide grid toggle has no expanded state, and prompt pages skip from h1 to h3. The ID field blocks password managers. The search field border is 2.998:1, just under 3:1.

    Fix: one attribute or one CSS line each; the details are in the accessibility audit.

    Accessibility

  19. Text-spacing and zoom edge cases

    #/dars/1–3 · RU/EN at 320

    With larger text spacing, the play circle in the folder footer is clipped. The library's empty search says "nothing found" twice.

    Fix: let the footer wrap, and hide the counter at 0.

    AccessibilityFinish

  20. Motion leftovers

    login · toast · accordions

    The welcome loader spends half its time fading in and cuts hard to home. The toast and popup appear with keyframes and vanish in one frame. Reduced motion also kills the gentle fades that help people notice overlays.

    Fix: a 160 ms loader fade-in and a 200 ms fade-out over home, matching enter and exit fades on overlays, and opacity-only fades for reduced-motion users.

    Motion

  21. Lesson 2 asks students to tick "I finished the library"

    #/dars/2 · Amaliyot

    Nobody finishes a 47-prompt reference, so lesson 2 can stay at 2/3 forever.

    Fix: count it done after the first copied prompt, extending copy = done to the library.

    UX

  22. Some 2.0 lines can cause wrong actions

    Vazifa sheet rule 3 · exercise 01 P4 · lesson ledes

    "…qolganini oʻchiring" can read as "delete the sources". Lesson 2 and 3 intros promise practice that 3.0 doesn't have.

    Fix: a content edit for the course owner: "belgisini olib tashlang (oʻchirmang)". Confirm the ledes with the mentor.

    UX

Your call

These change how the platform looks, so they wait for your decision. Ranked by impact.

  1. Fill the empty lesson tabs

    #/dars/1–4 · Slaydlar and Kitob panes

    About 85% of the biggest coloured shape on every lesson page is empty, because the Slaydlar tab holds one label and one button. Vazifa shows how to fill it: one huge "5/8" numeral.

    Proposal: the same layout for every tab: the slide position ("14/40") as the big numeral, with the action bottom-right.

    VisualFinish

  2. Make "Keyingi qadam" the strongest thing on home

    #/dastur

    The one next action is an 18 px line under the ring, and on most days the right half of the desktop home is empty.

    Proposal: the reference's outline pill with the circle arrow, placed in the empty right column; full width on phones.

    Visual

  3. Calm the login screen

    #/kirish

    The ID field still has a glow underline (red on error), a bouncy curve and a 10 px slide. The wordmark wave loops forever, uses about 15% of a mid-range phone's processor while the student types, and fails WCAG 2.2.2. "SHAXSIY KABINET" dissolves into the bright wave on phones.

    Proposal: a plain 2 px underline, the wave plays 2–3 times and stops, and a soft dark halo behind the headline. The photo stays.

    VisualMotionAccessibility

  4. Replace the 3D server-stack loader

    login → home

    It's the only shaded 3D object in a flat product, with red and green blinking lights, and it reads as stock art.

    Proposal: a black screen with "Xush kelibsiz, {ism}!" in the display face, optionally the yellow ring drawing once.

    VisualFinishMotion

  5. Remove the background grid

    every app screen

    The taste profile lists grids under "Never", but the tokens still draw a 5% grid and a floor gradient. With the glows gone, it is the last ambient decoration.

    Proposal: pure black, as in the reference, and update tokens.md so the two docs agree.

    VisualFinish

  6. Use the desktop width on inner pages

    Vazifa · exercises · prep · Hermes at 1440

    Sub-pages are one 770 px column, leaving 42–60% of the screen black, and the copy circles sit 300 px away from the text they copy.

    Proposal: reuse the lesson page's two-column split above 1200 px: header left, file groups right.

    VisualFinish

  7. Give lesson 4's dark card an edge

    #/dastur · #/dars/4

    #161616 on black is 1.16:1, so the folder shape disappears and the card reads as a hole in the list.

    Proposal: a 1 px #3F3F3F outline, the same hairline the nav uses. The colour stays.

    Visual

  8. Fix the library grid

    #/dars/2/amaliyot · 1024+

    Five cards in a 3+2 grid leave a hole that looks like a missing sixth section, and the cards are lilac (lesson 1's colour) on lesson 2's page.

    Proposal: home's 2+3 span rule, and decide whether section cards follow lesson 2's grey.

    Visual

  9. Show which fields a library prompt needs

    #/dars/2/amaliyot/*

    This goes beyond the copy toast in "Do next".

    Proposal: one line under each collapsed title, such as "Toʻldiring: [Soha]".

    UX

  10. Drop the gooey filter while a tab slides

    #/dars/n · desktop

    Only needed if the 260 ms timing fix in "Do next" still stutters.

    Proposal: a plain indicator while it moves, with the filter back on when it lands. This also fixes Safari, where the tab joint jumps today.

Keep

What every lens agreed the platform does well.

  1. It reads as Praktikum, not a template

    all five lenses

    Fixed lesson colours, folder cards, 88 px headlines and full-bleed lilac prompt lists are used consistently. There are no slop components: no gradients on content, no fake data, no 3-card feature rows. The exercise page is the clearest screen in the product.

  2. The colour system is fully AA

    accessibility · 42 routes × 4 widths

    0 text-contrast failures, measured both from styles and on real pixels; the lowest pair is 6.12:1. State is never shown by colour alone: there is always a ✓ or a word.

  3. Localisation and semantics hold

    finish · accessibility

    There is no raw ʻ/ʼ in 1,347 captures, and no horizontal scroll at 320 on any route. Course text is tagged Uzbek inside RU and EN. Tabs, dialogs, menus and live regions are built correctly, and reduced motion is fully honoured.

  4. Feedback is real and specific

    UX · motion

    Every gate error says what to do. Copy gives "Nusxalandi", marks the prompt and offers 5 s of undo. Locked and unknown links land on home with a reason. The prep pictures play once, pause off-screen and respect reduced motion. There are no console errors anywhere.
