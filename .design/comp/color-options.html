Rang tizimi audit · palitra · ma’no xaritasi · HOZIR → tuzatish · 3 variantTo‘liq hisobot: .design/color-audit.md

A colour audit of the three comps: 2 (flat cards + glow), 3 (flat cards + white threads) and Glass (smoked glass over state-colour art). The palette stays the same: black, blue, grey, yellow and lilac. What changes is that each colour carries one meaning, and every state is also written as a word (and an icon where one fits).

Ratios are WCAG 2.2. On glass and threads they were measured from the rendered pixels: the text was hidden, the page screenshotted, and each text colour composited over the real pixels under its glyph boxes (worst 5th percentile). Red marks a fail: under 4.5:1 for body text, under 3:1 for large text, UI parts and focus. HOZIR snippets copy the comps' literal values. Glass snippets paint the real art from glass-folder.js. Static snapshots: nothing is clickable.
