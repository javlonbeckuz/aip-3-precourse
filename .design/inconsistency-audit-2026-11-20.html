AI Praktikum 3.0: inconsistency audit

2026-11-20. Read-only pass over the student portal: SPA sources in .design/comp/src/, the build in public/aip/, content/ (lesson JSON, schedule, every PDF, read with PyMuPDF), and the screenshots in .design/d3-build/ (current) and .design/audit-2026-11-20/ (older). Each finding was checked against the source or a screenshot. Things that tokens.md or taste-profile.md mark as intentional are left out (each lesson's fixed colour, Uzbek lesson content in the RU UI, the "Kit" brand word). The pill and chip pattern inside the Amaliyot, Kit and Vazifa panes is being redesigned, so pane items only get the short list at the end.

Severity: High students notice it or get confused · Medium visible polish · Low minor. Line numbers point to .design/comp/src/ unless a path is given.
