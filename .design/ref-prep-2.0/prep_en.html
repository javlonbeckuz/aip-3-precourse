Before the first lesson

The first lesson is 12 September. Have this list ready by then — it takes about 30 minutes in all, and it means we start working straight away in class.

Download the PDF
01

A computer

A MacBook is recommended, Windows works too. A phone or a tablet is not enough.

  1. Bring your own laptop — the praktikum is hands-on from beginning to end.
  2. Bring the charger as well.
  3. Leave at least 5 GB free on the disk.
02

A Google account

Claude, NotebookLM and Gemini all open through this account. If you already have one, you do not need a new one.

  1. Open accounts.google.com/signup.
  2. Enter your first name, last name and a new email address.
  3. Confirm your phone number — a code arrives by SMS.
  4. Write the password down: you will need it in class.
Create a Google account
03

Claude

The main tool of the praktikum. Open an account on claude.ai first, then install the app.

  1. Go to claude.ai and sign up with "Continue with Google".
  2. Download the app from claude.ai/download.
  3. Mac: open the .dmg and drag the Claude icon into Applications.
  4. Windows: run the .exe and confirm the installation.
  5. Sign in to the app with that same Google account.

04

A Visa card

You will need it for a Claude Pro subscription. The free tier is enough for the first lesson.

  1. Get an international Visa or Mastercard from your bank — Uzcard and Humo do not work here.
  2. The card needs a dollar account with online payments enabled.
  3. Pro costs $20 a month. It is not required, but it makes the work considerably easier.

You will need it in the later lessons.
