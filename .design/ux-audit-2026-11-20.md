# UX audit: AI Praktikum 3.0 student portal, 2026-11-20 (eve of the course)

- **Walked:** the current build (`main` at `dac9d74` plus the white PDFs) on four local servers with pinned clocks: 20 Nov 18:00 (eve), 21 Nov 10:30 (lesson day, 01-dars and Kirish open), 20 Dec (05-dars live), 10 Jan 2027 (after the course). Students: `XM` as a plain shanba student, and a CRM-created student with **no group**. Headless Chrome at 1440×900 and 390×844 (mobile emulation), UZ and RU. Spot checks on the live site (ai.birfoizbilim.uz) and a read-only count of live students per group.
- **Measured:** an in-page probe on 52 pages (visible text under 15px, targets under 44px not widened by `::after`, horizontal overflow, broken images, console errors, HTTP errors). Every linked file in lessons 1-7 and Kirish was downloaded through the API.
- **Screenshots:** `.design/audit-2026-11-20/` (`NN-screen-desktop.png` / `-phone.png`).
- **Severity:** P1 = blocks or misleads a student. P2 = slows or confuses. P3 = polish. **Effort:** S < 1 h, M = half a day.

## Clean
No horizontal overflow at either width or in RU. No broken images, no console errors (the only 4xx is the expected 401 for a wrong ID). All linked files are real (the Kirish placeholders were replaced and deployed today). Live Kirish has no slides section (production fails closed), so the local "78 slides" placeholder never reaches students.

## Before Sunday 22 Nov (live: 27 active students are in yakshanba, 1 in shanba, 1 has no group)

### 1 · P1 · A student with no group is locked out with a date that has passed · S (data) + S (copy)
- **Where:** home, every lesson. Shots `16-nogroup-home-*`, `16-nogroup-l1-*`.
- **What:** on 21 Nov at 10:30 the no-group student still reads "Kurs 21-noyabrda boshlanadi" and "6 ta dars · 21-noyabrda ochiladi". Nothing ever opens and nothing tells them why. **One active student on the live site has no group right now.**
- **Fix:** set that student's group today (the CRM, or by hand in the DB). In the app, once the first start date has passed and the student has no group, replace the date line with "Guruhingiz hali belgilanmagan. Mentoringizga yozing." (and the same on the locked-lesson sheet).

### 2 · P1 · Students keep the old interface for up to 4 hours after each deploy · S (host)
- **Where:** live, `/aip3/app.js` and `/aip3/app.css` are served with `Cache-Control: max-age=14400`.
- **What:** after a deploy the browser runs the old `app.js` against the new API (seen on 9 Nov: the "Darsdan keyin siz" outcomes were live in the API but invisible).
- **Fix:** `Cache-Control: no-cache` for those two files in the nginx site of ai.birfoizbilim.uz (the repo's own server already does this, `server/index.js:357`), or a version query in `index.html`.

### 3 · P1 · Kirish never says which of its 7 tracks to do · S
- **Where:** Kirish › Amaliyot. Shots `12-kirish-amaliyot-*`.
- **What:** seven tracks and about 30 files. The content marks them "ASOSIY", "03B BILAN BIRIDAN BIRI", "03A BILAN BIRIDAN BIRI", "QOʻSHIMCHA", "NAMOYISH YOKI UYGA VAZIFA", but `noteParts()` (`app.js:1523`) strips that tag and nothing renders it. A student sees seven equal stops and cannot tell what is required, what is either-or and what is optional.
- **Fix:** render the tag as a small label next to the stop title (e.g. "Asosiy", "03A yoki 03B", "Qoʻshimcha", "Namoyish yoki uyga vazifa"). Lessons 1-7 tag every stop "ASOSIY", so either hide the label when every stop has the same tag, or show it only when it differs.

## P2

### 4 · Kirish files show raw file names, and six of them share the same name · S-M (content)
- **Where:** Kirish › Amaliyot and Vazifa.
- **What:** none of the 38 Kirish files has a `label`, so chips read "Talaba_qollanmasi.pdf", "Biznesimda_7_kunlik_sinov.pdf" and break mid-word on a phone. Every track has "01_Yoriqnoma.pdf" and "02_Promptlar.pdf": a student who opens two tracks gets two downloads with the same name ("01_Yoriqnoma (1).pdf"). Lessons 1-7 already use human labels (S6-8).
- **Fix:** add `label` ("HR · Yoʻriqnoma", "HR · Promptlar", "Talaba qoʻllanmasi", ...) and a distinct `name` per track ("01_HR_Yoriqnoma.pdf") in `content/lessons/2/lesson.{uz,ru}.json`.

### 5 · Kirish handouts still describe the 2.0 offline package · M (content owner)
- **Where:** `qollanma.pdf` and the Vazifa intro.
- **What:** the guide says to open "00_BOSHLASH.html", copy from "02_Promptlar.html" and upload "only the Manbalar folder"; none of these exist on the portal (the prompts are chips, the files are separate downloads). The Vazifa intro says "Oʻquv sanasi: 12.09.2026" and every handout footer reads "II VARIANT / OʻQUV NAMUNASI / 12.09.2026".
- **Fix:** one sentence in the Kirish Amaliyot intro that maps the guide to the portal ("Yoʻriqnoma va promptlar har mashqning ostida; Manbaalar = shu mashqdagi fayllar"), and decide whether the 12.09.2026 scenario date stays (it is the case's "today", so it can).

### 6 · Before the course the optional page is the first and brightest card · S
- **Where:** home on the eve. Shot `02-home-eve-*`.
- **What:** "Birinchi darsgacha" (Protokol 57 practice and the NotebookLM video) is the yellow first card and the header button; the required setup (computer, Google, **Visa card**, **Claude Pro**) is the second card. Both say "21-noyabrgacha".
- **Fix:** put "Oʻrnatish va tayyorgarlik" first; rename the other card, e.g. "Qoʻshimcha: mashq va video".

### 7 · The login help never says where the ID comes from · S
- **Where:** login. Shots `01-login-*`.
- **What:** "IDʼingizni kiriting" under an "AIP-" field. The CRM issues the ID after the first payment; a student who lost it has no hint where to look (10-19 #11, still open). The error lines themselves are good ("Bu ID topilmadi. Mentoringizdan tekshirib oling.", "IDʼda 0, 1, I, L va O boʻlmaydi").
- **Fix:** "ID toʻlovdan keyin [SMS / Telegram] orqali keladi" with the real channel.

### 8 · Mentor accounts read that lesson 1 "already happened" · S
- **Where:** Oʻrnatish va tayyorgarlik, for review accounts (XM on the live site).
- **What:** "Birinchi dars 21-noyabr kuni boʻldi". `courseStarted()` (`app.js:1130`) means "lesson 1 is open", and review accounts open it early.
- **Fix:** compare the lesson 1 start date with the clock instead.

### 9 · Small targets and small text (carry-over from 10-30 S0-1)
- **Targets:** the inline "Kit boʻlimida" link (103×21) on lesson pages; the video controls on Birinchi darsgacha (mute 24×24, fullscreen 36×36).
- **Text under 15px:** card and lesson meta ("21-noyabr · 0/2 bajarildi", 13px), the tab labels AMALIYOT / KIT / VAZIFA (13px), "3 ta qadam" (13px), "Shaxsiy ID" on login (12px).

## P3

### 10 · The locked-lesson sheet promises parts a lesson does not have
"Slaydlar, amaliyot, kit va vazifa dars kuni shu yerda chiqadi" appears for every lesson; 01-dars has no slides and Kirish has no Kit. Build the list from the lesson's `has`.

### 11 · There is no "course finished" state
On 10 Jan the home has no hero and still offers "Keyingi qadam: Oʻrnatish, Google akkaunt". After the last lesson, replace the next-step pill with a closing line (results, where the materials stay, what comes next). Shot `30-after-home-*`.

### 12 · The header pill keeps saying "Birinchi darsgacha" all course
It stays in the header on 21 Nov and 20 Dec. After the first lesson, rename it (e.g. "Qoʻshimcha materiallar") or drop it from the header.

### 13 · The Kirish Vazifa opens with five paragraphs of rules
About one phone screen of text before the first file. Fold the rules into "Qoidalar va yordam" as the Amaliyot pane already does.
