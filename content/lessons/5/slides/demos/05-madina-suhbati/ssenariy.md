# Demo 05: the staff conversation, role-played (2:21-2:26)

Curriculum Day 4 demo 5: "The Dilnoza conversation. Instructor role-plays the 15-minute Kit 4 talk with a mentor playing a worried employee."
In 3.0 the demo company is Baraka Office, whose HR roster has Dilnoza Murodova as head of HR, not as someone whose work the sales assistant changes.
So the worried employee is **Madina Sattorova** (showroom adviser), whose Kit 4 forms are the worked example (`../../exercises/namuna/kompaniya/kit4-people/madina-form-b.md`).
Deck slide 41.
Instructor notes in English; the dialogue is in Uzbek and is said aloud.

## Roles

- **Instructor** = Rustamov S.A., owner and general director.
- **Mentor** = Madina Sattorova.
  Brief the mentor 10 minutes before class with the card below.
- Two chairs facing each other at the front, no laptop.
  A printed copy of the updated written commitment in the instructor’s hand.

## Mentor card (Madina)

- You have worked here almost two years, you are good with customers, and you heard from a colleague that "the boss is bringing a robot".
- Bring up two worries, in this order, and do not agree at once:
  1. «Bot hamma narsani bilsa, keyin menga nima ish qoladi?»
  2. «Bot xato qilsa-yu, men payqamasam, mijoz oldida men aybdor bo‘lamanmi?»
- Relax only when the owner (a) shows the written commitment and (b) says plainly who answers for the bot’s mistakes.
- If the owner jokes or says "hozircha", look worried and say «Hozircha?».

## The dialogue (about 5 minutes; the real conversation is 15)

> **Rustamov:** Madina opa, 15 daqiqa vaqtingiz bormi? Ertaga yangi yordamchini yoqmoqchimiz. Oldin bu haqda Siz mendan eshitishingizni xohladim.
>
> **Madina:** Ha, eshitdim, robot olib kelyapsiz, deyishdi...
>
> **Rustamov:** Robot emas, Telegram’dagi yordamchi. Nima o‘zgarishini aytay: narx, ombor qoldig‘i va muddat haqidagi savollarga javobni endi u qoralaydi. Siz savolni unga yozasiz, javobni o‘qiysiz va mijozga o‘zingiz aytasiz. Otabek akaga yozib, yarim soat kutish yo‘qoladi.
>
> **Madina:** Bot hamma narsani bilsa, keyin menga nima ish qoladi?
>
> **Rustamov:** Mijoz bilan gaplashish Sizda qoladi: chalkash savoldan nima kerakligini Siz ajratasiz, bot emas. 1-dekabrdagi Optima mijozini eslaysizmi? «140 lik stolmi?» deb qayta so‘ramaganingizda, noto‘g‘ri stolni hisoblardik. Botga ham aynan shu kerak: aniq savol. Va mana yozma majburiyatim: may oyining oxirigacha bu loyiha sabab hech kimning maoshi ham, lavozimi ham o‘zgarmaydi.
>
> *(gives her the paper; pause; let her read)*
>
> **Madina:** Bot xato qilsa-yu, men payqamasam? Mijoz oldida men aybdor bo‘lamanmi?
>
> **Rustamov:** Yo‘q. Bot xatosi uchun men javob beraman, uchinchi bandda yozilgan. Siz har javobda beshta narsani tekshirasiz: mahsulot kodi, necha dona, hisob, muddat, chegirma. Bir daqiqa ham ketmaydi. Xato ko‘rsangiz, ❌ ni bosasiz va bir gap yozasiz. Xatoni topgan odam - yordamchini yaxshilagan odam: buning uchun rahmat aytaman, ayblamayman.
>
> **Madina:** Showroomda mijoz oldida telefonga yozib o‘tirsam...
>
> **Rustamov:** «Bir daqiqa, aniq narxni tekshiraman» deysiz. 14 sahifalik PDF’dan qidirishdan tezroq. Yana bir narsa: bot noto‘g‘ri ishlayotganini ko‘rsangiz, Malikaga ayting, u telefondan bir so‘z bilan to‘xtatadi.
>
> **Rustamov:** Yana nima xavotirga solyapti? *(waits, listens, writes in her words)*
>
> **Rustamov:** Kelishdik: maosh va lavozim o‘zgarmaydi; bugun soat 11 da Malika bilan 30 daqiqa mashq; tejalgan vaqt showroomdagi mijozlarga ketadi; 19-dekabr, shanba, birga ko‘rib chiqamiz. To‘g‘rimi?
>
> **Madina:** To‘g‘ri.

## Debrief with the room (1 minute)

UZ: "Nimani payqadingiz? Madina opa qaysi gapdan keyin tinchlandi?"
Take two answers; make sure these come out:

1. The order: the talk happens **before** the bot is switched on.
2. What stays the same was said with paper, not just words.
3. The owner did not argue with the worry; he answered it and wrote it down (B.8).
4. "Siz", "opa", no "optimizatsiya", no "bo‘shagan vaqt", no "hozircha".

## If it goes wrong

- The mentor overplays and it becomes comedy: stop, say "Haqiqiy xodim kulmaydi, u jim qoladi", restart from the second worry.
- Running late: cut the showroom exchange; keep the two worries and the agreement.
