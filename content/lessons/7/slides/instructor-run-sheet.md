## What today must deliver

The sales page promises for lesson 6: "Oldin va keyin raqamlari aniqlanadi, keyingi jarayon rejasi tuziladi" (`sources.md` S2).
Every owner leaves with four things:

1. A one-page results sheet (`results-sheet/`), checked by the mentor and signed: baseline vs pilot, hours and so'm, error rate, cost and payback, the decision.
2. A continue / change / stop decision in one sentence, measured against the success criterion (Kit 5 A.6) and the stop condition (A.7) written before the pilot.
3. Process #2 named from their own Kit 2 matrix, with the people they will interview first.
4. A presentation done at the table (and 8 of them on stage).

The definition of done (curriculum line 112, mentor check 6.9): one small tool, connected to at least one real system, used by a colleague for 2 weeks, with Kit 5 numbers compared to the baseline.

- The same rule for everyone; doubts are 0.5 with a note, never a quiet 1 or 0.
- Cells are never deleted or rewritten after today; a correction happens only through a dispute decision (within 3 days), as a new dated note.
- The signed results sheet is the record of "what changed": it shows the numbers, the verdict and the decision on one page, and its PDF path is in the evidence column.
- Post-course homework (`homework.md`) is not part of the 19 tasks.
- Open for the owner: whether an honest "to‘xtatish" counts as "a change in the business" for the guarantee, and when claims are checked (decisions README, question 5).
